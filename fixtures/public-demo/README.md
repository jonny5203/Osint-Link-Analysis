# Public demo evidence process

The deterministic fixture under `fixtures/demo/` is the default for development and
automated tests. Public people, organizations, vessels, or addresses must not be added
to that fixture.

Before adding an optional public-demo fact, create a review record containing:

- the fact-level source URL;
- the UTC retrieval date;
- a short quoted or closely paraphrased summary of the specific fact;
- the proposed graph nodes and relationship, including direction;
- the source-record or citation identifier attached to the relationship; and
- the reviewer's name, approval decision, and approval date.

Only public facts approved for redistribution may be committed. A name match or
similarity score is not sufficient evidence for an identity or relationship claim.
Remove secrets, personal contact details, and unrelated source content before review.
